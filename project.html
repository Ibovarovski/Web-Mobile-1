Project 1: How does hardware work?

Description

This team project was focused on understanding how computer hardware performs logical operations at a fundamental level. The goal was to design, build, and test basic digital logic gates using physical electronic components rather than simulations. During the lab sessions, we constructed NOT, AND, and OR gates on a solderless breadboard using NPN transistors, resistors, push buttons, LEDs, and a power supply module. Each circuit was assembled from the ground up, tested for correct input–output behavior, and adjusted when necessary. We also analyzed how current flow and transistor switching enable logical decision-making in hardware. The project combined teamwork, lab safety, and problem-solving, while reinforcing the connection between theoretical logic concepts and their real-world hardware implementation. Overall, this work demonstrates how simple electronic components serve as the building blocks of modern digital systems.
